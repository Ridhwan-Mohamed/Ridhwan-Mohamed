I’m interested in the complete spatial-computing pipeline: capturing real environments, recovering camera motion and 3D structure, representing scenes with point clouds or Gaussian splats, and visualizing the results efficiently on the GPU.

I currently work mainly with **C++20, Direct3D 12, HLSL, Python, OpenCV, point-cloud data, and 3D Gaussian Splatting**. My current learning focus is camera geometry, Structure from Motion, point-cloud registration, visual odometry, and eventually SLAM and persistent AR.

### Projects

✨ **[DirectX 12 Gaussian Renderer](https://github.com/Ridhwan-Mohamed/DirectX12-Gaussian-Renderer)**

A custom C++20 and Direct3D 12 renderer for trained 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes. It loads Inria-format PLY data and implements covariance projection, screen-space Gaussian evaluation, degree-3 spherical-harmonic colour, GPU visibility culling, radix depth sorting, indirect drawing, and alpha compositing.

🗺️ **[D3D12 Real-Time LiDAR Renderer](https://github.com/Ridhwan-Mohamed/D3D12-Realtime-LiDAR-Renderer)**

A point-cloud renderer for exploring large LAS, LAZ, and COPC geospatial datasets. It includes spatial level of detail, frustum culling, tile streaming and residency, GPU memory budgeting, classification and elevation visualization, and GPU timing.

📷 **Computer Vision and 3D Reconstruction Studies**

Current work with OpenCV and COLMAP covering camera intrinsics and extrinsics, feature matching, epipolar geometry, pose estimation, triangulation, Structure from Motion, and point-cloud registration. The goal is to connect captured images directly to spatial representations and real-time viewers.

🌌 **[Gravity Forge — DirectX 12 Galaxy Renderer](https://github.com/Ridhwan-Mohamed/DirectX12-Galaxy-Generator)**

A GPU particle simulation and renderer exploring compute shaders, GPU-driven visibility, HDR rendering, bloom, frame resources, synchronization, and rendering architecture.

🖥️ **[3DFX.js](https://github.com/Ridhwan-Mohamed/3DFX.js)**

A software 3D renderer built without WebGL or an external graphics API. Implements triangle rasterization, clipping, UV texturing, depth buffering, backface culling, matrix transformations, and 3MF model loading.
